Meridian Capital Partners — Investment Advisory Overview

Meridian Capital Partners is a registered investment advisory firm
managing approximately $340 million in assets under management for high
net worth individuals and family offices.

Investment approach: we build diversified portfolios across public
equities, fixed income, and alternative investments, with a focus on
long-term compounding rather than short-term trading. Typical client
portfolios hold 60-80% equities and 20-40% fixed income, adjusted for
each client's risk tolerance and time horizon.

Regulatory compliance: we are registered with the SEC and subject to
regular compliance audits. All client communications and trade
recommendations are archived per regulatory retention requirements, and
we maintain a strict fiduciary duty to act in clients' best interests.

Fee structure: we charge an annual management fee of 0.75% to 1.25% of
assets under management, depending on portfolio size, with no
performance fees or hidden trading costs.

Client base: primarily individuals with $1 million or more in investable
assets, corporate executives with concentrated stock positions needing
diversification strategies, and small family offices seeking outsourced
portfolio management.

Reporting: clients receive quarterly performance reports and have access
to a secure portal showing real-time portfolio holdings, performance
versus benchmark, and realized/unrealized gains for tax planning.

Common client concerns include tax-efficient rebalancing, estate planning
coordination with their attorneys, and risk management during market
volatility.
