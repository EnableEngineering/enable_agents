Ledgerly — Automated Accounts Payable for Growing SaaS Companies

Ledgerly is a B2B SaaS platform that automates the accounts payable
workflow for finance teams at companies with 50-500 employees. We
integrate directly with QuickBooks Online, Xero, and NetSuite to
eliminate manual invoice entry and approval-chasing.

The problem: finance teams at growing companies spend an average of 14
hours per week manually entering vendor invoices, chasing down approvers
over Slack and email, and reconciling payments at month-end close.
Late payments damage vendor relationships and duplicate payments are a
constant risk when invoice data lives in email inboxes instead of one
system of record.

How Ledgerly works: vendors email invoices to a dedicated Ledgerly
address, or upload them directly. Our OCR and line-item extraction reads
the invoice, matches it against purchase orders when available, and
routes it through a configurable approval chain based on amount and
department. Approved invoices sync directly to the customer's accounting
system for payment, with no manual re-entry.

Results customers report: 80% reduction in manual invoice processing
time, close cycles shortened by 3-5 business days, and a documented audit
trail for every approval, which matters a lot during SOC 2 audits.

Pricing: starts at $499/month for up to 200 invoices, with volume-based
tiers above that. No setup fee, and most customers are live within one
week of signing.

Target customer: VP of Finance or Controller at a Series B-D SaaS or
tech-enabled services company, currently using QuickBooks Online, Xero,
or NetSuite, processing more than 100 vendor invoices a month by hand.
