Apex Precision Machining — Capability Statement

Apex Precision Machining is a CNC machining supplier based in Ohio, USA,
specializing in precision aluminum and stainless steel parts for the
automotive and aerospace industries. Founded in 2009, we operate a
15,000 square foot facility with 12 CNC milling centers and 6 CNC lathes.

Certifications: ISO 9001:2015 and AS9100D certified. All aluminum parts
are produced to tolerances of +/- 0.001 inches.

Capacity: We can produce up to 10,000 units per month for a single part
number, with typical lead times of 3-4 weeks for new tooling and 1-2
weeks for repeat orders.

Materials: 6061 and 7075 aluminum, 304 and 316 stainless steel, titanium
Grade 5, and various engineering plastics (Delrin, PEEK).

Quality control: Every batch is inspected using coordinate measuring
machines (CMM) and first-article inspection reports are provided for all
new part numbers. We maintain full material traceability from raw stock
to finished part.

Minimum order quantity: 50 units for prototype runs, 500 units for
production runs. Custom tooling costs are quoted separately and are
typically amortized over the first production run.

Our largest customers include Tier 1 automotive suppliers producing
transmission housings and aerospace subcontractors producing structural
brackets. We are actively seeking new customers in the electric vehicle
and medical device sectors who need precision-machined aluminum
components with fast turnaround.
