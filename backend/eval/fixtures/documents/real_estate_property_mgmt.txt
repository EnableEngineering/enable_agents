Summit Property Group — Residential Property Management

Summit Property Group manages 1,400 residential rental units across 45
buildings in the Austin metro area, on behalf of both individual
landlords and institutional owners.

Services: tenant screening and leasing, rent collection, maintenance
coordination, and financial reporting to property owners. We use a
property management platform for online rent payment, maintenance
requests, and lease document storage.

Occupancy: portfolio-wide occupancy currently sits at 94%, with average
tenant turnover time (vacancy to re-leased) of 18 days, which we consider
a key operational metric owners care about most.

Maintenance: we coordinate a network of licensed contractors for
plumbing, electrical, and HVAC work, and respond to emergency maintenance
requests within 2 hours. Slow contractor response time during peak
seasons is a recurring source of tenant complaints.

Tenant relations: online maintenance-request submission has significantly
reduced phone call volume to our office, and tenants increasingly expect
to handle rent payment, lease renewal, and maintenance requests entirely
through a mobile app rather than calling or emailing.

Owner reporting: property owners receive monthly financial statements
showing income, expenses, and net operating income per property, and
increasingly ask for real-time dashboard access rather than waiting for
monthly PDF reports.

Regulatory: we stay current on local landlord-tenant law, including
security deposit handling and eviction procedures, which vary
significantly by jurisdiction within our service area.
