Riverside Family Clinic — Practice Overview

Riverside Family Clinic is a multi-specialty outpatient clinic serving
the greater Denver area, with three locations and 22 physicians covering
primary care, pediatrics, and cardiology.

Patient volume: approximately 18,000 unique patients per year across all
locations, with same-day appointment availability for urgent visits.

Electronic health records: we use a cloud-based EHR system and offer a
patient portal for scheduling, secure messaging with providers, and
viewing lab results. Patients frequently ask about wait times for
specialist referrals and how to access their own medical records.

Clinical focus areas: chronic disease management (diabetes, hypertension),
preventive care and annual wellness visits, and pediatric immunizations.
We are HIPAA compliant and maintain strict patient data privacy controls
across all systems that touch protected health information.

Staffing challenges: like most outpatient practices, we face ongoing
difficulty recruiting registered nurses and medical assistants, and are
evaluating technology that reduces administrative burden on clinical
staff so they can spend more time with patients.

Insurance: we are in-network with most major commercial payers and accept
Medicare and Medicaid. Billing and prior-authorization delays are a
recurring source of patient complaints.
