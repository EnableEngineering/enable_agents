Skillbridge Academy — Online Professional Certification Programs

Skillbridge Academy offers self-paced online certification programs in
data analytics, project management, and UX design, aimed at working
adults looking to change careers or advance in their current field.

Program format: each program runs 12-16 weeks, combines recorded video
lessons with weekly live cohort sessions led by an industry practitioner,
and culminates in a capstone project reviewed by an instructor.

Enrollment: approximately 3,200 active students across all programs at
any given time, with a completion rate of 68%, which is well above the
5-15% typical for fully self-paced online courses - we attribute this to
the live cohort component keeping students accountable.

Outcomes: 71% of graduates report a job change, promotion, or raise
within 6 months of completing a program, based on our own post-program
survey (not independently audited).

Pricing: programs range from $1,200 to $2,400, with income-share
agreements available for students who cannot pay upfront, and employer
tuition-reimbursement partnerships with several mid-size companies.

Student support: each cohort has a dedicated student success manager who
checks in weekly, and students report that falling behind on weekly
deadlines without early intervention is the single biggest reason for
dropping out mid-program.

Curriculum is refreshed twice a year to stay current with in-demand
tools and techniques, based on direct feedback from hiring-manager
partners in each program's field.
