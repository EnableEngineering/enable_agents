Coastal & Pine — Direct-to-Consumer Home Goods Brand

Coastal & Pine is a direct-to-consumer e-commerce brand selling
sustainably-sourced wooden home goods (cutting boards, furniture
accents, and kitchenware), shipping primarily to customers in the US and
Canada.

Sales channels: our own Shopify storefront accounts for 65% of revenue,
with the remainder split between our Amazon storefront and a small
wholesale program with independent home goods retailers.

Inventory: we hold inventory in a single 20,000 square foot warehouse in
Portland, Oregon, and typically carry 8-10 weeks of stock for our top 20
SKUs. Stockouts on bestsellers during holiday season have historically
been a significant lost-revenue problem.

Customer base: primarily women aged 28-45 interested in sustainable home
decor, with an average order value of $85 and a repeat purchase rate of
around 22% within 12 months.

Marketing: we rely heavily on Instagram and Pinterest for discovery, run
seasonal email campaigns to our list of 45,000 subscribers, and are
increasingly testing influencer partnerships with home-and-lifestyle
creators.

Fulfillment: average time from order to shipment is 1.5 business days,
and we offer free shipping on orders over $75, which is our single
biggest driver of average-order-value increases at checkout.

Sustainability is core to our brand positioning: all wood is FSC
certified and packaging is fully recyclable, which we highlight
prominently in product listings and customer communications.
